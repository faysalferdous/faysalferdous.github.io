DroidForensicX · Case study

When was the Android app updated?

An illustrated walkthrough using synthetic package records. All times are UTC.

Two dates appear in the record for org.example.notes. To reconstruct the sequence, pair each timestamp with its field name and compare them in the same time zone.

Inspect the sample record 
package: org.example.notes
firstInstallTime:
  2026-05-11 09:12 UTC
lastUpdateTime:
  2026-05-13 14:45 UTC
  1. 11 May · 09:12First recorded installation
  2. 13 May · 14:45Last recorded update

The recorded update came 2 days, 5 hours and 33 minutes after installation. Identifying who used the app, or when it was opened, would require separate evidence.
